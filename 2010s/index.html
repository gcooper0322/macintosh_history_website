The 2010s: when the Mac lineup... largely stayed the same.

By this point, much of Apple's profit was coming from their mobile devices, such as the iPhone and iPad; I won't go into those here because these pages are supposed to be about Macs. The only significant changes to the Mac lineup during this time involved the removal of internal DVD drives, the attempted switch to only having USB-C ports, and the introduction of "Retina" displays.
