The Power Mac G5 was the successor to the Power Mac G4, introduced in June 2003. It was the first Mac to use the PowerPC G5 CPU, and was marketed as "the world's first 64-bit desktop computer". Initial variants had one or two G5 CPUs at speeds between 1.6GHz and 2GHz; this was later progressively increased to 2.7GHz in the fastest variants. The very last revision also introduced a dual-core variant of the G5; the most powerful variant had two of these dual-core G5s running at 2.5GHz. The Power Mac G5 was discontinued in August 2006 in favor of the Mac Pro.
