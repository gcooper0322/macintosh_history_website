The Xserve was introduced in May 2002 as Apple's first rack-mounted server machine, intended as a file server or web server. The first variants shipped with 1GHz or 1.33GHz PowerPC G4 CPUs in single-CPU or dual-CPU configuration, up to 512MB of RAM, up to four 180GB hard drives, and a CD-ROM or CD-RW/DVD-ROM drive. Later variants used PowerPC G5 and Intel Xeon CPUs. The Xserve was discontinued in January 2011.
