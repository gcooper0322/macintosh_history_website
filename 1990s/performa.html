The Macintosh Performa series was Apple's main consumer and education Mac line beginning in September 1992. The Performa brand re-used models from Apple's other Mac series with model numbers that denoted included software packages or hard drive capacities, making for a generally convoluted lineup that included 64 different models over the name's lifespan and resulted in low sales due to consumer confusion. For that reason, no individual models will be mentioned here.
The Performa series shipped in many different cases, including "pizza box", horizontal, tower, and all-in-one desktop form factors, and used a wide range of CPUs from the Motorola 68030 and 68040 to the PowerPC 601, 603 and 603e. The Performa name was retired in March 1997.
