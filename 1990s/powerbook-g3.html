The PowerBook G3, initially the Macintosh PowerBook G3, was introduced in November 1997 as the first Mac laptop model to feature the PowerPC G3 CPU. The earliest variant had a 250MHz G3 CPU, and had a very similar design to the PowerBook 3400 that immediately preceded it; later variants gained an all-new, more rounded case design. Some variants also had options for multiple display sizes: 12-inch, 13-inch, or 14-inch. The third and fourth variants of the PowerBook G3 introduced an improved keyboard with translucent bronze-colored keys; these variants were also the first Mac laptops to have a glowing white Apple logo on the back of the display lid. The first variant sold for $5,700; later variants sold for between $2,299 and $3,499. The PowerBook G3 was discontinued in January 2001 and succeeded by the PowerBook G4.
