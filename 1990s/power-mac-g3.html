The Power Macintosh G3 was introduced in November 1997 as one of the first Macs to feature the PowerPC G3 CPU. It initially came in beige horizontal desktop or minitower cases for home or professional use, or as an all-in-one desktop for the education market, featuring the G3 CPU running at 233, 266, 300, or 333MHz, with prices starting from $1,599.
In January 1999, the Power Macintosh G3 switched to an all-new tower case in a bright blue-and-white color scheme, with the CPU clocked at 300, 400, or 450MHz, at prices ranging from $1,599 to $2,999.
