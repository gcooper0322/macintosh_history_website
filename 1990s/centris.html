The Macintosh Centris series was a short-lived lineup introduced in March 1993 as a mid-range desktop featuring the 68040 CPU ("Centris" indicating the center of Apple's overall product line). Apple only released three computers under the Centris name - the 610, 650, and 660AV - before the name was retired in September 1993 and these models were rebranded as Macintosh Quadra models. All Centris models sold for around $2,500.
