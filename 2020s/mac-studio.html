The Mac Studio is, so far, the only all-new Mac desktop introduced in the "Apple silicon" era. Intended as a high-end desktop offering, it features the Max or Ultra variant of Apple's M-series processors in a case very similar to that of the current Mac Mini, but significantly taller, at 3.7 inches tall.
